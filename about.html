About Mavambo

New beginnings for rural communities.

A community-led rural innovation centre in Mhondoro District, Zimbabwe.

Our story

A practical model for rural transformation.

Mavambo means “new beginnings.” The Centre is being developed on 8 hectares as a demonstration, training and enterprise hub where communities can learn practical approaches to farming, livestock, water management, clean energy and rural entrepreneurship.

Instead of short-term relief, Mavambo focuses on sustainable systems that can strengthen household incomes, food security and community resilience.

Our vision

To become Zimbabwe’s leading rural innovation centre, demonstrating practical solutions that improve livelihoods, strengthen food security and promote sustainable economic development.

Our mission

To empower rural communities through sustainable agriculture, water security, renewable energy, vocational training, entrepreneurship and climate-smart development.

Guiding principles

How we intend to work.

Community Ownership

Programmes are shaped around real local needs and practical participation.

Transparency

Responsible management, measurable outcomes and clear donor reporting.

Sustainability

Solutions are designed for long-term environmental and financial viability.

Inclusion

Women, youth, farmers and schools are central to the Centre’s mission.

Innovation

Modern technology is combined with local knowledge and practical learning.

Partnership

Government, civil society, business and communities can achieve more together.

Empowering rural communities through sustainable agriculture, water security, renewable energy, practical skills and entrepreneurship.
