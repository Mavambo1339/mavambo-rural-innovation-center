Founder’s message

A vision born from purpose.

Mavambo means “new beginnings.” It was established from the belief that rural communities can thrive when they have reliable infrastructure, practical knowledge and fair opportunities.

Our aim is to build a demonstration and training centre that strengthens food security, supports enterprise and inspires the next generation of rural innovators.

Spencer Ratsauka
Founder & Director
